Penyelesaian & Perhitungan Lama Tanggap

1. FCFS / PTPD (First-Come, First-Served)

Arah Proses (Gantt Chart):

| A (0-6) | B (6-14) | C (14-18) | D (18-25) | E (25-30) |

Waktu Selesai: A=6, B=14, C=18, D=25, E=30

Lama Tanggap (Turnaround Time = Waktu Selesai - Saat Tiba):

  • A = 6 - 0 = 6
  • B = 14 - 3 = 11
  • C = 18 - 5 = 13
  • D = 25 - 8 = 17
  • E = 30 - 12 = 18
Total Lama Tanggap = 65 | Rata-rata = 65 / 5 = 13
